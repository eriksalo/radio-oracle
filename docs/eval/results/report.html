What got fixed

The right book opens

Search candidates are now scored: exact title first, words in front of the request cost ("Two" Little Women, "A doctor enjoys" Sherlock Holmes), books by an author beat books about them, and a small table of canonical works and each author's best-known novel breaks ties. All 77 famous requests open the right book on the device; the confidence check now refuses the look-alikes.

Commands understood and resolved

Catalog questions ("Do you have any Springsteen?", "Any albums by the Beatles?", "Is there any jazz?") are exploration commands now, not Wikipedia questions. "Can you play X" is a request. "Song by Artist" is split, and the artist plays if the song isn't there. Tags are normalised (& / and, curly quotes, accents), whole-word artist matches beat substrings, and a nickname table knows "the Boss".

Modern sources for practical questions

Hits are merged with a per-collection bias by question type: WikiMed forward for medical, iFixit and Wikibooks for how-to, Gutenberg pushed back unless the question is about a book. Answers with a Gutenberg passage in their sources: 43 → 21. The prompt also labels Gutenberg passages as pre-1930.

“When should I plant tomatoes in Colorado?”
Sow seeds up to mid-July for a fall crop.
After the last frost, late spring, once the soil is 60 °F; start indoors at elevation.
“How do I tell if a mushroom is poisonous?”
A bitter taste often signals poison; blue-turning flesh is toxic.
There's no reliable way to tell by looking. If you find one, don't eat it.

Memory stays in the background

The four literature answers that ended with "You were on chapter 43 of Moby-Dick two days ago, HARK!" no longer do. Memory is used only when the user refers to it or asks about the very book in it.

No clock, no news

"What year is it?" now gets "I don't have a clock or calendar." The weather question is declined cleanly. The news question still mentions the 2024 article it retrieved before saying it can't know what's happening now.

Clean text reaches the voice

Runs of non-Latin script are stripped before speaking and storing. When the reply hits the token cap, it is cut back to the last complete sentence and any clause already queued past it is skipped before it plays. The tail-drop is now confirmed wired for llama-server (24 of 100 replies hit the cap in pass 3, all ended on a full sentence).

What still misses

Plot facts from a 4B model

"What happens at the end of A Tale of Two Cities" and "Summarize Treasure Island" are wrong in every run, even with the right article and the book's own text retrieved. The musketeers flip between runs. This is model capacity, not retrieval. Options: a larger model for literature questions only, or a per-book plot summary pre-computed on the workstation and indexed as its own passage.

Medical answers and "seek help"

Three wordings were tried. Too strong and the model turns fatalistic ("no doctor, no hospital, wait for help that doesn't exist"). Too soft and "seek medical help" creeps back into a few answers. The current wording gives steps first and keeps the referral to a mild aside in five answers; the bone-setting question still gets a refusal plus splint steps.

Length

The prompt asks for forty to fifty words; the model gives about seventy. The 120-token cap trims the longest to a clean sentence, so the median is 66 words now (was 72). Going shorter means cutting content, not style.

Sampling noise

At temperature 0.6 the same question gets a different answer each run, and about one in twenty flips between right and wrong. For a factual librarian, 0.3 to 0.4 would make answers repeatable; not changed here so the measured runs stay comparable.

Recommended next

  1. Lower the temperature to 0.4 and rerun once to confirm it reduces the flip-flops.
  2. Pre-computed plot summaries for the canonical works, indexed as one passage each, so plot questions retrieve a summary instead of the book's first chapter.
  3. Try the 8B model for literature only if memory allows a swap on demand; the 4B model's plots are unreliable regardless of context.
  4. Ask for confirmation before opening any book the confidence check rejects; it now rejects the look-alikes correctly.

Question sets, all four runs' raw results and hand grades are in docs/eval/results/; rerun with scripts/eval_quality.py.
